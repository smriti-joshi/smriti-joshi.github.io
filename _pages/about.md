<p class="about-intro">Building AI that clinicians can trust &mdash; and that works for everyone.</p>

I'm a PhD Candidate at <a href="https://www.bcn-aim.org/"><b>BCN-AIM</b></a>, Universitat de Barcelona, developing robust and trustworthy AI for medical imaging. My work sits at the intersection of **domain adaptation** and **uncertainty estimation** &mdash; making breast MRI AI that holds up across hospitals, scanners, and populations.

Before my PhD, I was a **Research Fellow at <a href="https://vcg.seas.harvard.edu/">Harvard</a>** (VCG SEAS) and hold an **Erasmus Mundus MSc** in Image Processing and Computer Vision from three European universities. I co-organized <a href="https://mama-mia.grand-challenge.org/"><b>MAMA-MIA @ MICCAI 2025</b></a> and served on the MICCAI 2024 LMIC committee &mdash; helping 50+ early-career researchers from lower-income countries access the global AI community.
